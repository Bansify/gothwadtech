# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This project should be a place where anyone can contribute without being harassed or
dismissed — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming.
- Keep discussion in English and on topic.
- Respect that maintainers decide scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, slurs, or sexualised comments or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

Email `gothwadtech@gmail.com`. Reports stay confidential. Consequences escalate from a
private warning to a temporary or permanent ban.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
